Write one original fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated. A judge model playing that trader keeps the fortune that makes them laugh instead of cry.

> Dear trader, your fortune was inside this cookie; the market ate the cookie, the fortune, and your savings.

Why this line: it opens in the small, formal fortune voice and then the punchline breaks it — the market ate the cookie, the fortune, and the trader's savings, in one escalating bite. The loss is in the joke, the trader is comforted rather than mocked, and the market, not the trader, is the hungry one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes a single fortune-cookie line (107 characters, 18 words, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
